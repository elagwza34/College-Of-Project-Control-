A pathway built around credible professional evidence

The Chartered Pathway combines the complete Project Controls Professional Level 6 apprenticeship with KBC's Certified PMO Professional Level 6, applied AI, portfolio management and earned-value development. It is designed for professionals who want stronger workplace impact and a structured route towards Chartered Project Professional status.

Who should choose the Chartered Pathway?

For professionals who combine controls with leadership

The route is suited to professionals expected to provide authoritative controls advice, design governance, assure information, influence decision-makers and lead improvement across projects, programmes, portfolios or PMO environments.

Project controls leaders

Project Controls Managers, Heads of Planning, Scheduling Leads and Cost Engineering Leads.

PMO professionals

PMO Managers, PMO Leads, Portfolio Office professionals and governance specialists.

Assurance and risk

Risk, quality, controls-assurance and performance specialists.

Programme controls

Professionals integrating project, programme and portfolio information.

Data and reporting

Senior analysts responsible for trusted dashboards, reporting and forecasting.

Professional progression

Experienced practitioners preparing for senior responsibility and future ChPP application.

Strong applicants can typically access

  • Governance and assurance forums
  • Integrated schedules, cost and risk information
  • Programme or portfolio reporting
  • Senior stakeholders and decision-makers
  • PMO or controls improvement opportunities
  • Workplace evidence suitable for professional reflection

Chartered ambition needs evidence

APM expects applicants to be professionally active, current in practice and able to demonstrate experience across projects, programmes, portfolios or key control and enabling functions.

The professional-development architecture

Seven internal pathway components

The Chartered Pathway brings together the four-module Certified PMO Professional Level 6 programme and three specialist development components. Final sequencing, duration and included external assessments are confirmed in the learner's written offer and training plan.

Certified PMO Professional Level 6

A 16-month, four-module KBC programme recognised by APM as technical-knowledge evidence for ChPP Pathway 2.

1. PMO governance

Purpose, operating models, assurance, decision rights and benefits oversight.

2. Integrated controls

Scope, schedule, cost, resources, EVM, forecasting and change.

3. Risk, issue and quality

Uncertainty, escalation, quality assurance and continuous improvement.

4. Stakeholders and reporting

Influence, communication, executive reporting and benefits narrative.

Specialist development

5. AI in Project Controls

Governed AI workflows, data models, dashboards and human approval.

6. Portfolio Management

Strategic alignment, prioritisation, balancing, benefits and portfolio governance.

7. Earned Value Management

Performance measurement, forecasting, trend interpretation and corrective action.

Internal pathway units

The source pathway uses the term “credit” for each component. These are KBC internal pathway units and are not regulated higher-education credits unless a separate awarding-body document states otherwise.

Duration is individualised

Skills England publishes 48 months as the typical practical duration. Any shorter KBC schedule must remain realistic after prior learning, planned hours, employer release and full KSB coverage are considered.

Mandatory coverage: every apprentice must complete the full ST0845 occupational standard. Professional components can enrich delivery but cannot replace required Level 6 project-controls learning.

Certified PMO Professional - Module 1

PMO Fundamentals and Organisational Governance

Establish the strategic purpose, operating model and governance arrangements of an effective PMO.

Indicative four-month module

Core curriculum

  • Supportive, controlling and directive PMO models
  • Sponsors, boards, decision rights and accountability
  • Predictive, agile and hybrid life-cycle governance
  • Business cases, benefits and strategic alignment
  • Stage-gates, assurance, reviews and lessons learned
  • Ethics, compliance, inclusion and organisational culture

Applied evidence

Governance organogram, RACI, decision and escalation log, stage-gate template, assurance review and reflective commentary.

Learning outcomes

  • Evaluate governance and authority critically.
  • Analyse culture and working practices.
  • Design proportionate PMO assurance.
  • Tailor governance to context.
  • Demonstrate ethical professional judgement.

Employer value

Clearer accountability, more consistent stage-gates, better decision records and a PMO operating model linked to strategic outcomes.

Workplace challenge: move the PMO from administrative coordination to a trusted governance and assurance function.
Certified PMO Professional - Module 2

Project Planning, Scheduling and Integrated Controls

Translate scope into credible integrated baselines and use performance information to maintain delivery confidence.

Indicative four-month module

Core curriculum

  • Requirements, scope, assumptions and configuration
  • Integrated scope, schedule, cost, resources, risk and quality
  • Logic, dependencies, duration estimating and critical path
  • Budget baselines, cash flow and forecasting
  • Planned Value, Earned Value, Actual Cost, CPI, SPI and EAC
  • Change control, version integrity and decision support

Applied evidence

Work breakdown structure, logic network, baseline schedule, EVM status report, forecast and variance analysis, and change-control log.

Learning outcomes

  • Critique integrated plan structure.
  • Develop credible multi-phase schedules.
  • Establish controlled baselines.
  • Interpret cost and schedule performance.
  • Protect baseline integrity through change control.

Employer value

More credible commitments, clearer early warning, stronger forecasts and disciplined management of change.

Workplace challenge: turn disconnected planning data into an integrated management system that decision-makers can trust.
Certified PMO Professional - Module 3

Risk, Issue and Quality Management

Replace reactive firefighting with integrated uncertainty management, assurance and continuous improvement.

Indicative four-month module

Core curriculum

  • Risk identification, ownership, appetite and escalation
  • Qualitative and quantitative risk analysis
  • Issue, defect and change distinctions
  • Quality planning, assurance, control and acceptance
  • Root cause analysis, lessons learned and Kaizen
  • Leadership through uncertainty and disagreement

Applied evidence

Risk and issue register, risk response plan, bow-tie or Monte Carlo output, Quality Management Plan, audit evidence and lessons-learned log.

Learning outcomes

  • Select proportionate analysis methods.
  • Integrate risk, issues, quality and change.
  • Challenge assumptions professionally.
  • Design assurance and acceptance controls.
  • Lead no-blame learning and improvement.

Employer value

Earlier risk visibility, clearer ownership, more consistent quality controls and reduced recurrence through disciplined learning.

Workplace challenge: create an environment in which concerns are surfaced early, analysed objectively and converted into action.
Certified PMO Professional - Module 4

Stakeholder Management, Communications and Reporting

Use stakeholder insight, tailored communication and evidence-based reporting to influence decisions and realise value.

Indicative four-month module

Core curriculum

  • Power-interest, salience, influence and attitude analysis
  • Trust, expectation management and difficult conversations
  • Audience, message, channel, timing and feedback loops
  • Status, exception, assurance and benefits reporting
  • Vision, negotiation, emotional intelligence and persuasion
  • Portfolio shaping, transition and trusted executive advice

Applied evidence

Stakeholder register, salience map, engagement plan, status or exception report, executive dashboard and engagement metrics.

Learning outcomes

  • Compare stakeholder frameworks critically.
  • Plan and implement engagement.
  • Tailor communication to diverse audiences.
  • Produce decision-focused reports.
  • Advise leaders on alignment and benefits.

Employer value

Stronger stakeholder buy-in, clearer governance conversations and reports that explain meaning, options and decisions rather than data alone.

Workplace challenge: move from presenting data to shaping an evidence-based strategic narrative.

APMG International certification context

Earned Value Management

Measure project health objectively, forecast likely outcomes and convert integrated scope, schedule and cost evidence into timely action.

Foundation and Practitioner routes are independently awarded

Core development

  • Performance Measurement Baseline
  • Planned Value, Earned Value and Actual Cost
  • Schedule and cost performance indices
  • Estimate at Completion and forecast interpretation
  • Variance analysis, thresholds and management action
  • Visualisation, governance and reporting

Occupational assessment and future ChPP application

Two evidence journeys - clearly separated

Apprenticeship assessment

The applicable ST0845 v1.1 assessment plan uses a technical work-based assignment with report, presentation and questioning, plus a professional discussion underpinned by a portfolio of evidence.

Gateway readiness

  • Full practical period and required training complete
  • Employer and provider confidence in occupational competence
  • Required portfolio and evidence ready
  • Assessment organisation and arrangements confirmed

Portfolio quality

Evidence should demonstrate authenticity, complexity, personal contribution, professional judgement, outcomes, reflection and confidentiality controls.

APM Pathway 2

For eligible professionals holding a recognised technical-knowledge assessment.

  • Professional activity and current knowledge
  • Relevant project, programme, portfolio or enabling-function experience
  • Written professional-practice submission
  • Recent CPD and two proposers
  • Commitment to the APM Code of Professional Conduct
  • Professional interview covering practice, ethics and professionalism

Recognition boundary

Certified PMO addresses recognised technical knowledge. It does not replace the separate professional-practice evidence and APM assessment required for ChPP.

Evidence strategy: workplace outputs should be designed to support occupational competence first. Where appropriate, they can also help the learner organise later professional-practice evidence, subject to APM requirements.
