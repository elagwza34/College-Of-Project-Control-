Technical knowledge. Professional practice. Chartered ambition. A pathway built around credible professional evidence The Chartered Pathway combines the complete Project Controls Professional Level 6 apprenticeship with KBC's Certified PMO Professional Level 6, applied AI, portfolio management and earned-value development. It is designed for professionals who want stronger workplace impact and a structured route towards Chartered Project Professional status. K Technical knowledge Build advanced understanding of governance, integrated controls, risk, quality, stakeholder leadership, PMO and assurance. P Professional practice Apply learning to authentic project environments and organise credible evidence of judgement, autonomy and impact. E Ethics and CPD Develop professional conduct, reflective practice, current knowledge and an evidence-led improvement mindset. C Chartered readiness Understand APM Pathway 2 expectations and prepare a stronger professional evidence base for a later independent application. section {'id': 'pathway-structure', 'class': 'kbc-section', 'aria-labelledby': 'pathway-structure-title'}
The professional-development architecture Seven internal pathway components The Chartered Pathway brings together the four-module Certified PMO Professional Level 6 programme and three specialist development components. Final sequencing, duration and included external assessments are confirmed in the learner's written offer and training plan. Certified PMO Professional Level 6 A 16-month, four-module KBC programme recognised by APM as technical-knowledge evidence for ChPP Pathway 2. 1. PMO governance Purpose, operating models, assurance, decision rights and benefits oversight. 2. Integrated controls Scope, schedule, cost, resources, EVM, forecasting and change. 3. Risk, issue and quality Uncertainty, escalation, quality assurance and continuous improvement. 4. Stakeholders and reporting Influence, communication, executive reporting and benefits narrative. Specialist development 5. AI in Project Controls Governed AI workflows, data models, dashboards and human approval. 6. Portfolio Management Strategic alignment, prioritisation, balancing, benefits and portfolio governance. 7. Earned Value Management Performance measurement, forecasting, trend interpretation and corrective action. Internal pathway units The source pathway uses the term “credit” for each component. These are KBC internal pathway units and are not regulated higher-education credits unless a separate awarding-body document states otherwise. Duration is individualised Skills England publishes 48 months as the typical practical duration. Any shorter KBC schedule must remain realistic after prior learning, planned hours, employer release and full KSB coverage are considered. Mandatory coverage: every apprentice must complete the full ST0845 occupational standard. Professional components can enrich delivery but cannot replace required Level 6 project-controls learning.
