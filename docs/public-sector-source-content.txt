Employer questions Questions before you enquire. Can an existing public-sector employee complete an apprenticeship? Yes, where the apprenticeship provides substantial new learning, the employee’s role supports application and all current eligibility requirements are met. Which route is right for project support or coordination roles? Associate Project Manager Level 4 is typically the more relevant route for employees coordinating delivery, supporting governance, managing workstreams and developing broad project-management capability. A formal review is still required. Who is the Project Controls Professional Level 6 route designed for? It is intended for employees whose role requires integrated control across schedule, cost, risk, change, performance reporting and governance, such as planners, cost professionals, risk specialists, controls engineers and PMO controls teams. Are the apprenticeships fully funded? They may be potentially fully funded through an appropriate route, but this depends on the employer, learner, programme, prior learning, available funding and current rules.
