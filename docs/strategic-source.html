Who should apply?

For professionals who use controls evidence to influence decisions

The pathway is designed for experienced professionals with access to suitable strategic activity, credible workplace evidence and employer support.

Project-controls leadership

Professionals responsible for integrated controls, performance, forecasting and delivery confidence.

Heads of project controls Project controls managers Senior planning and cost professionals Risk and assurance practitioners

Programme and portfolio roles

Professionals connecting project evidence with programme outcomes, investment priorities and organisational capacity.

Programme-controls professionals Portfolio governance specialists Transformation professionals Benefits and investment roles

PMO and delivery leadership

Professionals shaping governance, assurance, decision support and delivery capability.

Heads of PMO PMO managers Strategic delivery leaders Governance and assurance professionals

Role suitability: a senior job title is not enough. The role must provide a substantial link to ST0845, suitable workplace activity and enough scope to develop significant new occupational competence.

Eligibility criteria

Could this apprenticeship pathway be right for you?

Kent Business College completes a role, suitability, prior-learning and funding review before enrolment. Use this guide and quick check as an initial indication only.

1

UK resident for the past 3 years

You must have lived in the UK for the past 3 years.

2

Must not require sponsorship to work

You must hold a British Passport, Indefinite Leave to Remain, or a Tier 2 visa with at least three years of UK residency.

3

Not enrolled in other government-funded training

You must not be enrolled in other government-funded training at the time of this programme.

4

Self-employed individuals are not eligible

Self-employed individuals are not eligible for DfE funding.

5

Paid employment in England

You must be in paid employment in England, normally 30+ hours per week, with a minimum of 16 hours.

6

Employer based in England

Your employer must be based in England and registered with the Apprenticeship Service.

7

Spend at least 50% of working hours in England

You must spend at least 50% of your working hours within England.

This is an indication only and does not guarantee eligibility, funding or enrolment. Final suitability is confirmed after KBC's full role, prior-learning, employer-support and funding review.
